Redraw the little ambient critters of my game, Tales of Dorlune: a cute top-down fantasy RPG for iPhone (pixel-art world, chibi characters, bright and cosy). They wander, hop and fly about the lands for life; you don't fight them. The attached _8x pictures are each critter as it is now, every frame, at 8×. Today they're simple and stiff. Make them adorable: the look of the best tiny pixel-art pets, chunky round bodies, big heads, big shiny eyes with a white catchlight, a hint of blush, a clear silhouette, a soft personality in the pose.

Style rules, all of them matter:
- Real pixel art on the exact canvas listed for each critter (width × height, the same as today, so the game places them as before), transparent, alpha 0 or 255 only, no anti-aliasing, no blur.
- A one-pixel outline in a deep shade of each part's own colour (dark brown round a brown bunny, deep green round a frog), never pure black; lit from the top left, three or four tones a colour, warm hue-shifted shadows; 8 to 20 colours a critter.
- Seen from slightly above and to the side, as the game's world is; facing right (the game flips them to walk left).
- Every frame of a critter on the same canvas with its feet on the same bottom row and its body in the same place, only what moves moving (legs, wings, ears, a head going down to graze or peck), so the frames loop without the critter jumping. A colour variant (a cream bunny, a blue butterfly) is the same drawing as its first, only recoloured.
- Original drawings only: nothing copied from another game or asset pack.
