- So a sheet is 9 frames in one row: 576 × 64 pixels for a 64-pixel boss (the 40 × 40 ones 360 × 40).
- In every frame the boss stands with its feet or base on the same row, about 2 pixels above the frame's bottom, centred, the same size in every frame.
