# New buildings for every land, by ChatGPT (2026-11-21)

Not redraws: buildings the game has never had, two for each land, so each land's buildings are its own. The towns
and lands mostly build with a handful of Retro Diffusion houses and their recolours (`house`, `house_green`, the
mushroom houses, `inn_teal`, `bakery`...), the same in Meadowbrook as in Prismhaven.

## How to run it

One chat per batch below, in the ChatGPT project (its instructions already cover pixel rules, outline, colours,
the two files and the checks). Attach `style_reference_4x.png` (eight of ChatGPT's own buildings already in the
game, at 4×) to every chat, paste the **prompt** with that batch's list in place of `[LIST]`, and send the PNGs
back here named by their id. Four a chat, as before.

The size is fixed for all of them, so no measuring: our houses are 96 × 96, the building 58 to 78 wide and 55 to
81 tall, standing on row 89 (`thatched_cottage` 78 × 64, `dome_house` 59 × 81, `tea_house` 78 × 55).

## The prompt

> Draw 4 new buildings for my game, Tales of Dorlune, one at a time, as the project's instructions say. They're
> new, not redraws: there's no picture of them, so don't measure. The attached sheet shows eight of our
> buildings at 4× on grey: match their look exactly (soft rounded shapes, chunky tinted outline, broad shading
> lit from the top left, cute and cosy, a little storybook).
>
> Every one: a 96 × 96 canvas, transparent; the building 58 to 78 pixels wide and 55 to 81 tall, its lowest
> pixel on row 89; seen from the front and a little from above, like the sheet; a front door you could walk up
> to, near the middle of its front; window panes in warm bright yellow that nothing else in it uses (no yellow
> signs, cloth, gold or highlights: the game lights the panes at night); 30 to 45 colours; no ground, slab or
> shadow under it (a few grass tufts or a doorstep at most). Draw it in natural colours: the game tints each
> land's art itself.
>
> [LIST]
>
> For each: say its size, foot row and colour count, then deliver its 1× PNG named by its id and the 8×
> preview, then the next.

## What happens to them

Each comes back here: a clean 1× at the size, scored (`pixel_style.py check --kind building`), a small outline
fix in code if needed (as for the redraws), its night windows cut (`window_art.py`, `BOXES` if anything else
lights), registered in `art/assets.json`, and given lots in its land (maps.json `buildings`, town `lots`). The
user picks by eye; what doesn't win isn't used.
